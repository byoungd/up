# Real Projects, Jobs, and Entrepreneurship: Turn Learning into Evidence

If you are searching for real project ideas, English job-search preparation, or a way to test a business idea, you do not need to finish a giant course first. Choose a specific person, constraint, and deliverable, then let feedback choose the next step.

## Who It Is For

- People who want learning to become a portfolio piece, case study, interview evidence, or first customers.
- People who need English for international teams, customer conversations, or remote applications.
- People willing to test an entrepreneurship hypothesis at low cost and hear a real “no.”

## Start in Three Steps

1. Choose a real problem from the [Practice Map: Start with a Problem](../practice.md), then define the user, deliverable, and completion criteria with the [Artifact Brief and Delivery Card](../templates/artifact-brief.md).
2. For job search, use [Job-search English and Remote Work](../threads/part-1/8-job-search-english.md) and the [Job-search English Evidence Card](../templates/interview-evidence.md) to turn a role description into evidence you can show.
3. For entrepreneurship, interview people with [From Problems to First Customers](../threads/practice/customer-discovery.md), then record the hypothesis, cost, feedback, and stop condition with the [Startup Experiment](../templates/startup-experiment.md).

## Next Step

After two weeks, do not ask only “Did I learn it?” Check whether a real person used the work, an interview answer became more specific, a customer continued the conversation, and the cost remains acceptable. Put the result into [AI Development and Resource-layer Business](../threads/part-3/2-ai-development-and-resource-layer.md) or [Recent Updates](../recent-updates.md) and start the next cycle.
