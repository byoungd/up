# AI Trend Experiment: From Signal to Decision

Test one change at a time. This is not a product scrapbook or a forecast leaderboard. It helps you decide whether a capability belongs in your task. Without a manual baseline, an inspectable result, and a stop condition, do not expand the scope.

## Order of Judgment

Check sources and permissions first, then facts and task outcomes, and only then compare speed, style, or charges. Separate recognition errors from knowledge errors. Separate a model's completion claim from the file, database, or real reader outcome. Once a held-out set informs tuning, move it into development or regression coverage and add fresh held-out cases.
